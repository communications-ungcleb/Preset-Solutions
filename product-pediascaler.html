Home / Products / PediaScaler

Exclusive, high-tech automated childcare examination table

PediaScaler measures a baby's height and weight instantly and displays the result on an interactive touch screen — no walking to another room, no juggling loose supplies. Every instrument the doctor needs stays within arm's reach.

Download Brochure Request a Demo
Why It Works

Benefits

  • Reduces stress and inconvenience of moving the infant patient
  • Rimmed sides secure active infants, restricting falls
  • Reduces the physician's back strain from repeated bending
  • Keeps the physician's hands free — no need to record results on paper
  • Lets parents follow live examination results on screen
  • Automatic paper roll keeps every exam hygienic between patients
Under the Hood

Features

  • Built-in infantometer with folding arm and wireless control
  • Digital video otoscope records inner-ear exams in real time
  • Touch controls for overhead light and X-ray viewer
  • Virtual keyboard for patient data entry, auto-archived to file
  • Easy-clean leather bed, lockable storage, auto/manual calibration
  • Customizable size, color and finish to match your clinic's decor
