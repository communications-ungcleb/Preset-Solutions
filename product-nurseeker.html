Home / Products / NurSeeker

A powerful, flexible integrated nurse call communications solution

The needs of hospitalized patients keep growing more complex. NurSeeker relays patient calls directly to the assigned caregiver's wireless phone, voice-recognition badge or pocket pager — so staff respond faster, without running back and forth to the nursing station.

Download Brochure Request a Demo
Why It Works

Benefits

  • Instantly directs patient calls to the right caregiver, wherever they are
  • Quieter healing environment by reducing overhead paging
  • Enhanced staff mobility and productivity
  • Fast, direct staff-to-staff communication — no more "phone tag"
  • Improved patient and staff satisfaction
Under the Hood

Built for hospital-grade reliability

  • Touchscreen nurse console speeds response and staff-to-patient assignment
  • Patient info auto-updates from the ADT system before staff answer a call
  • Asset-locating technology finds portable equipment instantly
  • Housekeeping / room-ready buttons automate bed turnover
  • Bed exit, I.V. pump and ventilator alarms route straight to the caregiver's device
  • UL1069-listed nurse call equipment
  • Life-safety core is not PC-based — no software virus exposure, 24/7 operation
  • Structured Cat 5/5e/6 cabling lowers install and service costs
