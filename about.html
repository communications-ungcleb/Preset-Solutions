About Preset Solutions

Our Preset Solutions, will reset your visions.

A Beirut-based engineering house building the telecom, security and low current systems that institutions run on — from the queue at the counter to the alarm on the roof.

Our Story

Built by engineers, not resellers

Preset Solutions was founded on a simple idea: the companies best placed to support a mission-critical system are the ones who designed it. Working out of Jdeideh, Beirut, our engineering team has spent years developing proprietary hardware and software — from Q-InServer, our queuing management platform, to NurSeeker, a full nurse-call communications system for hospitals.

That in-house model means every installation — a bank's teller line, a hospital ward, a call center floor — is backed by the same people who built the system, not a third-party manual. It's why over a hundred institutions across banking, healthcare, government, hospitality and industry keep coming back to us for the next building, the next branch, the next upgrade.
