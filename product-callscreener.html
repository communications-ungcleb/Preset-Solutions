Home / Products / CallScreener

An efficient, sharp solution for screening and monitoring calls

CallScreener is a live telephone call monitor that detects the operating status of every line — idle, ringing or busy — the instant it changes. USB modular hardware pairs with fully customizable software so supervisors see the whole switchboard at a glance, with chronometric timing on every line.

Request Brochure Request a Demo
Why It Works

Benefits

  • See every line's status — idle, ringing or busy — at a glance
  • Chronometric timing flags calls left ringing or on hold too long
  • Adjustable reminder sounds keep supervisors and agents alert
  • Scales from a small switchboard to a full call-center floor
Under the Hood

Features

  • USB modular, interchangeable hardware interface
  • Fully customizable software with selectable options and controls
  • Selectable audio wave files or voice-prompt notifications
  • Adjustable ringing reminder timing per line
